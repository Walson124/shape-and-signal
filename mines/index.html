How to play

  • Click a cell to open it. Right-click (or long-press on touch) to flag a mine. Hover a cell and press Space to flag it.
  • Click an open number when its mines are all flagged to open everything around it.
  • Every board starts with an open area, so nobody has to guess their first click.
  • Same board: everyone gets the same mines. Separate boards: everyone gets a different board with the same size and mine count.
  • Hitting a mine either freezes you for 5 seconds or knocks you out, whichever the host picked.
